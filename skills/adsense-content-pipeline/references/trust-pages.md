# Trust and Policy Pages

These pages tell a reviewer, and a reader, who runs the site and how it behaves. Boilerplate generated for someone else's site is worse than a short honest page. Use only facts the owner confirms, and mark every unknown `[TK: …]`.

Not legal advice. The owner is responsible for legal accuracy in their jurisdiction. Always check the live Google help pages linked below before publishing.

## Required or expected pages

| Page | Why | Maps to |
|---|---|---|
| **About** | Who runs the site, why, and what qualifies them. Real names or a real organisation. | `ADS-UX-05`, `ADS-PUB-05`; CORE-EEAT Ept01, T02 |
| **Contact** | At least one working channel, ideally two (email plus a form or address). Test it. | `ADS-UX-05`; CORE-EEAT T02 |
| **Privacy Policy** | Required. Must include the Google advertising disclosures below. | `ADS-PRIV-01`, `ADS-PRIV-02` |
| **Terms / Disclaimer** | Where relevant: tools, advice, YMYL topics, user accounts, UGC. | `ADS-UX-05`; CORE-EEAT T01, T08 |
| **Author pages / bylines** | A real person, what they actually know or have done, and a link to the About page. | CORE-EEAT Ept01–Ept02 |
| **Editorial policy** | How content is researched, checked, updated, and corrected; whether and how AI assistance is used. | CORE-EEAT T05, T06, Ept10 |
| **Affiliate / sponsorship disclosure** | Needed if there are any material connections. Put it near the links as well as on a page. | CORE-EEAT T04 (veto), `ADS-PUB-05` |

All of them must be linked site-wide, usually in the footer, and reachable without login.

## Privacy policy: the advertising disclosures

Google's AdSense policies require that the privacy policy disclose the data use that comes from Google's products. Check the current text at the source before publishing:

- Required content (privacy policy): https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348695?hl=en
- How Google uses data on partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdSense Program policies, privacy section: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en

As of writing, the disclosures cover:

1. Third-party vendors, **including Google**, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
2. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites.
3. Users may opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings.
4. If other ad vendors or networks are used, name them or link to them, and tell users how to opt out of those vendors' cookies (for example, via the vendors' own opt-out pages).
5. Other data the site itself collects: analytics, forms, comments, and newsletters. What is collected, why, how long it is kept, and who it is shared with.

Also cover, where applicable:

- **EEA / UK / Switzerland visitors:** Google requires a Google-certified CMP for consent (`ADS-PRIV-04`). Link: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116?hl=en
- **Children:** if the content is child-directed, it must be tagged and treated accordingly (`ADS-PRIV-06`).
- **Local law:** for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, GDPR, and US state laws. Flag these for the owner rather than drafting legal positions.

Never pass personal data (emails, names) in URLs or ad requests (`ADS-PRIV-03`).

## About page: what makes it real

- Who: the name(s), role, and a photo if the owner is comfortable with one.
- Why the site exists, and who it is for, in one short paragraph.
- What qualifies the authors: real experience, work, credentials, or an honest "we research and synthesise; here is how".
- How to reach them: a link to Contact.
- Never invent a team, an office, awards, media mentions, or years in business.

## Editorial policy: minimal honest version

- How topics are chosen, and which sources are preferred (primary sources first).
- How claims are checked, and who checks them (a real named person).
- How often pages are reviewed, and how corrections are made and shown.
- Whether AI tools assist drafting, and that a human reviews every page before publishing. Google suggests disclosing substantial automation where readers would reasonably wonder how a page was made.

## Output

For each page: its status (missing / weak / ok), the URL, what is missing, and a draft written with `human-web-content` that contains `[TK]` markers only where the owner must supply facts.
